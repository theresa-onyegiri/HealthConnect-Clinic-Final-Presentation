This project provided understanding of HealthConnect business challenges, uncovered hidden insights that aided further analysis to get context and clarity on the areas or segments that are contributing to the high rate of missed clinic appointments.

Furthermore, this project created a predictive model to help HealthConnect Clinic input patient's data during appointment booking to predict the customer's behavior and likelihood of missing the appointment. This is a solution to enable the business run future appointment predictions and take necessary actions ahead of time, to prevent missed appointments. 

This project also provided relevant business recommendations to enable HeathConnect Clinic make better business decisions and achieve overall business objectives and success.
